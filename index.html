What gets checked
  • Ticket vs DMR — each day’s quantity per product against the DMR’s “daily used” (billed-not-used, used-not-billed, quantity mismatch, product missing on either side).
  • Ticket integrity — Total column vs sum of the daily cells; pack size/unit vs DMR; hole section on that date.
  • DMR integrity — daily and cumulative stock balances, usage above available stock, stock carry-over and cumulative-used continuity between consecutive days, duplicate/missing/mis-numbered reports.
  • Coverage — days billed on the ticket with no DMR uploaded (shown as dashed days).
Product names are matched after ignoring case/punctuation, with aliases (e.g. “Chrome free Lignosulphonate” ↔ Spersene CFI, “Lubricant (Bit Lube)” ↔ Bit Lube).
